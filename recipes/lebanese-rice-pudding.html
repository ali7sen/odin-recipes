Lebanese Rice Pudding Recipe

Home

Description

Lebanese rice pudding, known as riz bi haleeb, is a creamy milk-based dessert or breakfast dish flavored with orange blossom water and sometimes rose water.

Ingredients

  • Rice: 1/2 cup short-grain rice
  • Liquids: 1 cup water + 5 cups milk (whole or low-fat, reserving 1/4 cup for cornstarch)
  • Sweetener: 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • Thickener: 3 tablespoons cornstarch (mixed with the reserved 1/4 cup milk)
  • Flavorings: 1 to 2 tablespoons orange blossom water (and optional rose water)
  • Toppings: Crushed pistachios, shredded coconut, or cinnamon

Steps

  1. Simmer the rice in water over medium-low heat for 5–7 minutes until most water is absorbed.
  2. Stir in the milk and sugar, then cook gently for about 15 minutes, stirring often so it does not stick.
  3. Whisk in the cornstarch slurry and cook for ~5 minutes until thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in the orange blossom water (and rose water, if using)
  5. Pour into bowls or ramekins, cool slightly, and serve warm, at room temperature, or chilled.
